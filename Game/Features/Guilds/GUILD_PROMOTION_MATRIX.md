# Guild Promotion Matrix

This document outlines which ranks can promote to which ranks in the guild system.

## Legend
- ✅ = Can promote (with conditions noted)
- ❌ = Cannot promote
- ⚠️ = Special conditions apply (see notes)

## Promotion Rules

### General Rules
1. You can only promote members who have a **lower or equal rank** than yourself
2. You cannot promote someone who is already at the target rank
3. Guild Leader **cannot** promote to Manager or Supervisor (these are auto-assigned)
4. CP costs are deducted from the **promoting player's CPs** (not guild CPs)
5. All promotions are subject to rank limits based on guild level

## Notes

### <sup>1</sup> Guild Leader Transfer
- Guild Leader can transfer leadership to another member
- The current leader becomes Deputy Leader after transfer
- No CP cost required

### <sup>2</sup> Honorary Deputy Leader
- **Cost:** 650 Conquer Points (from promoting player's CPs)
- Subject to guild level limits
- Can only be appointed by Guild Leader

### <sup>3</sup> Manager & Supervisor Restrictions
- Guild Leader **cannot** manually promote to Manager or Supervisor
- Manager is auto-assigned to the player with the highest donation (1st place)
- Supervisors are auto-assigned based on donation/flower requirements

### <sup>4</sup> Honorary Manager
- **Cost:** 320 Conquer Points (from promoting player's CPs)
- Subject to guild level limits
- Can only be appointed by Guild Leader

### <sup>5</sup> Honorary Supervisor
- **Cost:** 270 Conquer Points (from promoting player's CPs)
- Subject to guild level limits
- Can only be appointed by Guild Leader

### <sup>6</sup> Honorary Steward
- **Cost:** 100 Conquer Points (from promoting player's CPs)
- Subject to guild level limits
- Can be appointed by Guild Leader

### <sup>7</sup> Lower Ranks Promotion
- Deputy Leader, H. Deputy Leader, and Leader Spouse can promote to:
  - Steward
  - Honorary Steward
  - Deputy Leader Aide
  - Follower
  - Member
  - Any rank below Steward and above Member (inclusive)

## CP Costs Summary

| Rank | CP Cost | Appointed By |
|------|---------|--------------|
| Honorary Deputy Leader | 650 CPs | Guild Leader only |
| Honorary Manager | 320 CPs | Guild Leader only |
| Honorary Supervisor | 270 CPs | Guild Leader only |
| Honorary Steward | 100 CPs | Guild Leader only |

**Note:** All CP costs are deducted from the **promoting player's personal CPs**, not from guild CPs.

## Rank Limits

All promotions are subject to rank limits based on guild level. See `GuildRankLimits.cs` for specific limits per guild level.

## Auto-Assigned Ranks

The following ranks are **automatically assigned** by the system and cannot be manually promoted:

- **Manager:** Assigned to the player with the highest total donation (1st place)
- **Supervisors:** Assigned based on donation/flower requirements:
  - Tulip Supervisor (TSupervisor)
  - Orchid Supervisor (OSupervisor)
  - CP Supervisor (CPSupervisor)
  - Arsenal Supervisor (ASupervisor)
  - Silver Supervisor (SSupervisor)
  - Guide Supervisor (GSupervisor)
  - PK Supervisor (PKSupervisor)
  - Rose Supervisor (RoseSupervisor)
  - Lily Supervisor (LilySupervisor)
- **Agents:** Assigned based on donation/flower thresholds
- **Followers:** Assigned based on donation/flower thresholds
- **Senior Members:** Assigned when Total Donation reaches 50,000

---

## Special Cases

1. **Leader Spouse:** Automatically appointed when the leader's spouse is in the same guild and is not a Deputy Leader
2. **Spouse Ranks:** Various spouse ranks (Leader Spouse, Deputy Leader Spouse, Manager Spouse, Supervisor Spouse, Steward Spouse) are auto-assigned
3. **Aide Ranks:** Can only be appointed by their corresponding superior rank:
   - Leader Spouse Aide → Guild Leader
   - Deputy Leader Aide → Deputy Leader / H. Deputy Leader / Leader Spouse
   - Manager Aide → Manager / H. Manager
   - Supervisor Aide → Supervisor / H. Supervisor / All Supervisor types
   - Aide → Agent

## Command Types

- **Command Type 28 (`Promote`):** Regular promotions without CP cost
- **Command Type 34 (`PromoteWithCP`):** CP-required promotions (Honorary ranks)
