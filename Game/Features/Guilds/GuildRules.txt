Guild Leader
1. Recruit new Guild members.
2. Appoint Deputy Leaders, Honorary officials, Leader Aides and all other officials except Manager and Supervisor.
3. Unlock Martial Arsenal.
4. Dismiss Guild members.
Note: It takes the Guild Leader's CPs (NOT Guild CPs) to appoint Honorary officials.
1) Honorary Deputy Leader: 650 CPs
2) Honorary Manager: 320 CPs
3) Honorary Supervisor: 270 CPs
4) Honorary Steward: 100 CPs
_________________________________________________________
Deputy Leader & Honorary Deputy Leader & Leader Spouse
1. Recruit new Guild members.
2. Appoint Stewards, Honorary Stewards, Deputy Leader Aides and other lower-ranking officials. There are no aides for Honorary officials.
3. Unlock Martial Arsenal.
4. Dismiss Guild members with positions lower than Steward (included).
Note: When the leader's spouse is in the same Guild as the leader and is not appointed as a Deputy Leader of this Guild, the spouse will automatically be appointed as the Leader Spouse.
_________________________________________________________
Manager
1. Recruit new Guild members.
2. Appoint Manager Aides. There are no aides for Honorary officials.
3. When a player's donation reaches the 1st place, he or she will be appointed as Guild Manager, automatically.
_________________________________________________________
Honorary Manager
1. Recruit new Guild members.
2. Appoint Manager Aides. There are no aides for Honorary officials.
_________________________________________________________
Supervisor
Appoint Supervisor Aides. There are no aides for Honorary officials.
Note: Guild members will be automatically appointed as a Supervisor when their donation or flowers received meet the requirements for a certain Supervisor. There are 9 types of Supervisors in a Guild, altogether.
_________________________________________________________
Donation Supervisor	& Honorary Supervisor
1. Recruit new Guild members.
2. Appoint Supervisor Aides. There are no aides for Honorary officials.
Note: Guild members will be automatically appointed as a Supervisor when their donation or flowers received meet the requirements for a certain Supervisor. There are 9 types of Supervisors in a Guild, altogether.
_________________________________________________________
Steward & Deputy Leader Spouse & Leader Spouse Aide & Deputy Leader Aide
Stewards are appointed based on their Total Donations.
Please view the Faction Official Numbers listed above, for corresponding Donation requirements.
When the leader's spouse is in the same Guild as the leader and is not appointed as a Deputy Leader of this Guild, the spouse will automatically be appointed as the Leader Spouse.
Note: Total Donation refers to the combination of Arsenal Donation, Silver Donation, CP Donation, Guide Donation and PK Donation.
_________________________________________________________
Deputy Steward
Guild members will be appointed as the Steward when their Total Donation hits 170,000. There is no limit to how many Stewards a Guild can have.
_________________________________________________________
Agent & Donation Agent & Supervisor Spouse & Manager Spouse & Supervisor Aide & Manager Aide
1. Guild members will be appointed as the Agent when their Total Donation hits 150,000. No limit per Guild.
2. A Guild can have 1 Charm Agent of a type of flower. The member with the most flowers of a type will become the Charm Agent for that flower. There are certain thresholds for each type of Charm Agent.
Rose Agent: At least 2,430 Roses received.
Lily Agent: At least 1,620 Lilies received.
Orchid Agent: At least 810 Orchids received.
Tulip Agent: At least 1,620 Tulips received.
3. A Guild can have 1 Donation Agent for each type of donation. The member with the most donations of a type will become the Agent for that donation. There are certain thresholds for each type of Donation Agent.
Silver Agent: At least 31,500.
CP Agent: At least 120,000.
PK Agent: At least 9,000.
Guide Agent: At least 9,000.
Arsenal Agent: At least 100,000.
Agents can appoint their own Aides

Follower & Steward Spouse & Donation Follower
1. Spouses of officials in this column will be appointed by the system, automatically.
2. Officials in these positions will be appointed by the system automatically, when their flowers received or donations reach the following amounts.
Follower & Senior Member: Total Donation = 80,000.
Rose Follower: 810 Roses received.
Lily Follower: 540 Lilies received.
Orchid Follower: 270 Orchids received.
Tulip Follower: 540 Tulips received.
Silver Follower: 15,000.
CP Follower: 40,000.
PK Follower: 3,000.
Guide Follower: 3,000.
Arsenal Follower: 33,000.
_________________________________________________________
Senior Member && Steward Spouse
Members will be appointed as Senior Members, when their Total Donations hit 50,000.
